4. DEMAND TARGET DEFINITION
------------------------------------------------------------

Target:

daily_demand

Definition:

Daily demand is calculated as the total quantity from
positive-quantity transactions aggregated by calendar date.

Negative quantities represent returns/cancellations and
are excluded from the demand forecasting target.

Formula:

daily_demand =
SUM(quantity WHERE quantity > 0)

Returns and cancellations remain in the cleaned transaction
dataset but are not included in the forecasting target.

Demand patterns were investigated using:

✓ Daily demand distribution
✓ Missing calendar dates
✓ Weekday demand patterns
✓ Monthly demand patterns
✓ Demand outliers
✓ Large transaction investigation
✓ Cancellation/return investigation
✓ Autocorrelation analysis


Important findings:

- Saturday had very low/zero demand.
- Weekday strongly influenced demand.
- Demand contained several large positive spikes.
- Large spikes were caused by bulk orders.
- Demand distribution was positively skewed.
- Sudden spikes were difficult to predict.
- Returns/cancellations could significantly distort

13. ERROR ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

The model performed reasonably well during normal demand
periods.

The largest prediction errors occurred during:

- Sudden demand spikes
- Unexpected bulk orders
- Rapid changes in demand


Potential factors unavailable in the dataset:

- Promotions
- Marketing campaigns
- Holidays
- Customer-specific bulk purchases
- External business events


These factors could explain sudden demand changes that
cannot be inferred from historical demand alone.
